# The Static  
*Genre: Supernatural Horror / Mystery / Thriller*  
*Setting: Maple, 2011 – a nostalgic small town clinging to analog media*

---

## I. Show Overview

### Tone & Vibe
- **Analog Horror Aesthetic**: VHS grain, flickering TV static, distorted audio.
- **Childhood Camaraderie & Isolation**: Echoes *Stranger Things*, with emotional depth and nostalgia.
- **Slow-Burn Mystery**: Family secrets, lost media, and forgotten trauma fuel an ever-deepening conspiracy.

### Core Mystery
A mysterious VHS tape and a broadcast known as “**The Signal**” begin appearing across local TV sets. After classmate **Megan Harper** vanishes, a group of 12-year-olds investigates. Clues lead them through forbidden tapes, encrypted radio frequencies, and an abandoned TV station. Watching “The Signal” may be tied to a dark pattern of disappearances spanning generations.

---

## II. World & Premise

### The Phenomenon
An unmarked VHS tape is discovered at school. When played, it’s just static—until a ghostly face appears. Soon after, local TVs display **“The Signal”**—a disturbing countdown accompanied by whispers and surreal imagery, seemingly connected to the town’s missing persons history.

### Maple
A sleepy, analog town filled with secrets. Key locations:
- **Maple Video** – last surviving VHS rental store.
- **WZTR Channel 9** – abandoned TV station.
- **Maple Archives** – dusty city records revealing disturbing patterns.

### Catalyst
The disappearance of **Megan Harper** and the clues she left behind pull **Noah** and friends into a chilling conspiracy.

## III. Main Characters & Families

### A. The Kids (Ages 11–12)

---

#### 1. Noah Hayes  
*The Leader / The Dreamer (12)*  
- **Traits**: Brave, creative, obsessed with horror filmmaking.  
- **Family**:
  - **Dad**: John Hayes – former documentarian, now an alcoholic haunted by the past.
  - **Mom**: Sarah Hayes – overworked nurse, misses emotional cracks at home.
- **Relationships**:
  - Childhood best friends with **Lena** (drifting apart).
  - Receives shy admiration from **Jade**.
  - Best buds with **Benji**.

---

#### 2. Jade Kim  
*The Introvert / Sensitive Tech Genius (12)*  
- **Traits**: Quiet, smart, sensitive. Finds escape in electronics and old VHS tapes.
- **Family**:
  - **Mom**: Grace Kim – owns Maple Video.
  - **Dad**: Absent since Jade was little.
  - **Brother**: Chris Kim (17) – fallen golden boy, involved in shady business.
- **Relationships**:
  - Secretly crushes on **Noah**.
  - Close emotional bond with **Lena**.
  - Quietly loyal to **Benji** despite teasing.

---

#### 3. Benji Torres  
*The Skeptic / Comic Relief (11)*  
- **Traits**: Anxious, sarcastic, skeptical but strangely sensitive to "The Signal".
- **Family**:
  - **Parents**: Strictly religious and dismissive of supernatural ideas.
  - **Sister**: Isabella Torres (16) – rebellious, shares contraband horror media with him.
- **Relationships**:
  - Best friends with **Noah** since kindergarten.
  - Has a crush on **Lena**.
  - Teased by **Jade**, but deeply values him.

---

#### 4. Lena Carter  
*The Sensitive One / The Believer (12)*  
- **Traits**: Intuitive, deeply emotional, believes in the supernatural.
- **Family**:
  - **Mom**: Emily Carter – works multiple jobs.
  - **Dad**: Abandoned when she was 5.
  - **Brother**: Elliot Carter (19) – ex-radio station tech, holds crucial knowledge.
- **Relationships**:
  - Formerly inseparable from **Noah**.
  - Deep, quiet connection with **Jade**.

---

## IV. Season One: Episode Guide (10 Episodes)

---

### Episode 1: Channel 99
- The kids find an unmarked VHS tape in Megan’s school desk.
- Static on Noah’s TV shows a fleeting image of Megan’s face.
- Introduction to “The Signal”.

---

### Episode 2: The Last Transmission
- Jade builds a device to decode a strange radio signal at 3:33 AM.
- The signal whispers phrases connected to Megan’s disappearance.

---

### Episode 3: Face in the Static
- Benji sees Megan’s face in the static, followed by a violent nosebleed.
- The group shares supernatural experiences and grows closer.

---

### Episode 4: Rewind
- Noah analyzes the VHS frame by frame—finds a shadow behind Megan.
- John’s drunken ramblings suggest he knows more than he’s saying.

---

### Episode 5: Vanishing Files
- Jade finds decades of missing persons reports tied to “The Signal”.
- Realization: This is bigger than just Megan.

---

### Episode 6: Echoes
- Lena has cryptic dreams and finds matching messages in a hidden diary.
- Her connection to the supernatural deepens.

---

### Episode 7: Dead Air
- The kids explore the old TV station—find notes warning about watching “The Signal”.
- Hints that the station was shut down after a dangerous broadcast.

---

### Episode 8: The Signal Grows
- The phenomenon spreads—mirrors, phones, town screens all show static.
- Adults begin noticing something is wrong.

---

### Episode 9: The Other Side
- The kids theorize “The Signal” is a portal.
- Detective Graves reopens old files. A shadowy door opens into an alternate Maple.

---

### Episode 10: The Last Broadcast
- The group fights back by creating a counter-broadcast at the old station.
- As the countdown hits zero, they confront the entity behind “The Signal”.
- Emotional sacrifices. Truths about Megan and their families are revealed.

---

## V. Themes & Visual Style

### Themes
- **Memory & Oblivion**: What happens when your past is erased?
- **Secrets & Trauma**: Families and towns hiding truths for decades.
- **Innocence vs. Corruption**: Youthful wonder corrupted by darkness.

### Visual Style
- **Analog Horror**: VHS grain, static, CRT glow.
- **Moody Color Palette**: Muted greens, reds, and yellows; warm suburban nostalgia meets surreal horror.
- **Diegetic Sound**: Cues from radios, tapes, and TVs to deepen unease.
